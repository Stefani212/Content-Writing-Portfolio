# Content Writing Portfolio

A selection of articles I wrote during my internship at Max1, where I developed my interest in content writing and gained practical experience in researching, structuring, and creating web content.

The articles presented here represent **only a selection of the content I worked on during my internship** and are included to showcase different types of topics, formats, and approaches I used.

## My Approach to Content Writing

During my internship, I had the opportunity to work on different topics related to the company's field. My first task was to propose 10 article topics based on my interests and the company's activities. After a topic was approved by a marketing specialist, I developed the article based on research and the requirements of the topic.

My first article was **“Will AI Replace Web Designers?”**. Through the following articles, I gradually developed my own approach to creating content.

One of the most important things I learned was the importance of reliable information. I believe that a good article should not simply sound convincing — the reader should be able to understand where a claim comes from and what it is based on. For this reason, I focus on researching topics and using reliable sources, facts, and statistics.

I also learned that useful content needs to be easy to understand and navigate. Depending on the topic, I use elements such as tables, internal and external links, images, and frequently asked questions to make information more accessible and useful to the reader.

## SEO & Content Structure

For me, SEO is not simply a set of technical requirements. The content should first be useful and understandable to the person reading it, and then structured in a way that makes it easier for search engines to discover and understand.

My approach includes:

* researching the search intent behind a topic;
* using reliable sources and supporting claims with facts;
* structuring content with clear headings and sections;
* using internal and external links where relevant;
* incorporating tables, images, and FAQs when they improve the user experience;
* creating content that is useful for both readers and search engines.
